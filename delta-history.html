ℹ️ How it works: History is stored as dated gear +/- events under delta_snapshots/gear_events/ (true day-over-day changes). Pick two dates below to aggregate events in that range. Legacy cumulative delta_daily_*.json.gz files are still loaded as a fallback when event shards are missing.
Default start/end prefers the latest snapshot and the newest prior file within 14 calendar days (so sparse archives do not open on a long gap by mistake).
