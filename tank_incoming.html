Assumptions

  • Death is client HP below -10. HP of -10 stays up on that check. A later heal does not revive a dead tank.
  • Swings in one round share a timestamp. A heal at that same time is applied after the attacks.
  • Jitter is uniform on the closed millisecond range from minus the setting to plus the setting.
  • Defense is the level-65 class cap. Item skill mods are not in the item file, so none are applied.
  • Quarm's enrage is on the database row and is not modeled, because it depends on Quarm's HP.
  • Area rampage hits this tank only under the one-target hate list branch in this source.
  • A finite horizon is not indefinite survival. Results stay estimated.
