How spell land rate works (TAKP)

This page documents the v1 calculator assumptions. The formula is a client→NPC port of Server Mob::CheckResistSpell in zone/spells.cpp with PoP-on rules (no classic softcap curve, no PvP bow curve).

Roll is always on 0–200 (201 discrete outcomes). Full land when roll > resist_chance. Roughly: 2 points of resist ≈ 1% on that scale.

Core formula

resist_chance = effective_target_resist + level_mod + ResistDiff

effective_target_resist = mob[MR|FR|CR|DR|PR] + sum(debuff deltas)
  • ResistDiff comes from the spell row (negative = easier to land).
  • Debuffs (Malo, Tashan, Harmony of Sound, chants) lower the mob’s resist stats; they do not change ResistDiff.
  • Lull / Harmony / Pacify casts override target resist to 15 (PoP+).
  • Enchanter mez/charm: if CHA > 75, ResistDiff -= (CHA-75)/8 (initial cast only).
  • Tick save: treats caster level as +4 (DoT ticks).

200-scale vs 600-scale

From IsPartialCapableSpell:

  • 600-scale (partial) — first non-blank effect is HP damage (SE_CurrentHP / SE_CurrentHPOnce with negative base), and the spell is not flagged no_partial_resist. Failed rolls can still deal reduced damage.
  • 200-scale (binary) — everything else (most debuffs, snares, mez). Fail = full resist.

The calculator reports exact full-land / full-resist / partial percentages by enumerating all 201 rolls. For 600-scale it also shows expected average effectiveness.

Level modifier (NPC)

Uses the Server NPC branch: quadratic level-diff term (floored at −9), ResistFalloff 67, high-level “bump” when the mob is well above the caster, and an extra +2 × level_diff for direct-damage spells.

Instrument mod (drum)

Bard resist songs use effect × mod / 10 with integer truncation (C++ style). Default drum 3.1 → mod 31. UI clamps multiplier to 1.0–3.8.

Example: Harmony of Sound base −15 → −15 × 31 / 10 = −46 to MR/FR/CR. Tuyen’s top chants base −22 → −68 at 3.1.

Debuff magnitudes

  • Malosinia −70 MR/FR/CR/PR (not disease); exclusive with Malos/Mala
  • Malos −55 MR/FR/CR/PR (not disease); does not stack with Malosinia
  • Mala −35 MR/FR/CR/PR (not disease)
  • Malicious Decay −52 DR
  • Howl of Tashan −50 MR
  • Harmony of Sound −15 MR/FR/CR × drum
  • Occlusion of Sound −10 MR/FR/CR × drum
  • Fufil’s Curtailing Chant −18 MR × drum (Lucy)
  • Tuyen’s top chants (Fire/Ice/Venom/Plague) −22 on matching resist × drum

Data

  • data/spells_resist.json — built from Server spells_en via scripts/build_spells_resist_json.py
  • data/resist_debuffs.json — toggle definitions
  • data/resist_calc.js — formula implementation

Mob resists are fillable only in v1 (no Zeal/NPC dump on the site). Plug in values from parses or in-game tools.

Out of scope (v1)

  • Classic-era resist softcaps
  • Focus effects that change ResistDiff
  • Sanctification / fear resists / rain innate 20%
  • Full NPC resist catalog
