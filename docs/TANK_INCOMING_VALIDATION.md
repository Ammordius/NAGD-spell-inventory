# Tank incoming

`tank_incoming.html` simulates one tank against one raid mob. Survival means HP stays at -10 or above for the fight length. Average DPS and HPS are diagnostics.

Encounters are generated by `scripts/build_tank_encounters.py`. It keeps mobs named in `raid_item_sources.json` that match a row in the Zeal `npc_info` zone JSON. Quest labels and slash lists are skipped. Names with no Zeal row stay in the list as upcoming expansion fights and are not given combat numbers. `attack_count` of 1 or less is one primary swing, matching `DoMainHandRound`. One special lands per round, in order: flurry, rampage, area rampage.

Defensive reduces every landed melee hit by 30%. The Divine Intervention clicky follows `TryDeathSave`: while the tank is still alive and under 16% HP it can heal 8000, then the clicky is down for 10 seconds. Charisma sets the chance. Heal timing skews late: from half the jitter setting early through the full setting late.

## Slow

`SE_AttackSpeed` in `zone/bonuses.cpp` does `real_slow_value -= real_slow_value * GetSlowMitigation() / 100`. At 100 mitigation the slow amount is 0, so `Mob::GetHaste` stays 100 and the slow is cancelled. Integer division stays: a 75% slow at 25 mitigation becomes haste 43.

## Avoidance

Lightning Reflexes is `SE_AvoidMeleeChance` added to `CombatAgility` in `ApplyAABonuses`. `Client::GetAvoidance` then does `rating + rating * percent / 100`. Rank 5 is +15% of the gear avoidance rating (defense and agility), not +15 points and not a flat miss chance.

Adding 100 to that rating is not 10% miss. `AvoidanceCheck` only moves the miss chance by about 10 points per 100 rating when to-hit is near 400. On Quarm, to-hit is `7 + 330 + 330 + 750 = 1417`, and +100 rating is a few points of miss. The page shows the miss change for the selected mob. Worn Mod2 avoidance stays the experimental miss-check percent and does not change ranking weights. The NPC `avoidance` column is the mob's bonus, not the tank's.
